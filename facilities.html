Our Facilities

Train On World-Class Ground

Good coaching needs the right environment. Our facility is built so training never has to stop — whatever the weather or the level.

Full-Size Grass Ground

A spacious, well-maintained grassy ground for match practice, fielding drills and full-scale simulations under open skies.

Turf & Matting Practice Nets

Multiple full-length nets across turf and matting surfaces, so players adapt their game to different conditions from an early age.

Bowling Machines

Pace and spin bowling machines allow batters to face high volumes of consistent deliveries — and build shot repetition fast.

Strength & Conditioning Zone

Age-appropriate fitness equipment and a dedicated trainer to build the athleticism modern cricket demands.
