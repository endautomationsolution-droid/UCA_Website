About Us

Coaching Cricketers Since 2017

Ultimate Cricket Academy was built on a simple idea: talent deserves structure. We combine old-school discipline with modern, video-backed coaching to help every player — from first-timer to future state player — reach their game.

Our Mission

To give every cricketer, regardless of where they start, access to structured coaching, honest feedback and the facilities needed to genuinely improve — not just play more overs.

Our Vision

To be recognised as the academy that consistently produces disciplined, technically sound cricketers who go on to represent their school, district and state with pride.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Discipline

Punctuality, preparation and effort — on and off the field.

Excellence

Constant, measurable improvement over quick shortcuts.

Integrity

Honest feedback about a player's game, always.

Teamwork

Cricket is personal skill built inside a team culture.
